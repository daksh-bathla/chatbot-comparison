CONVERSATION 1: Simple FAQ Question
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RULE-BASED CHATBOT:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
You: What are your hours?
Bot: We're open Mon-Fri 9am-6pm EST, Sat 10am-4pm EST. Closed Sundays.

You: Are you open on Saturday?
Bot: We're open Mon-Fri 9am-6pm EST, Sat 10am-4pm EST. Closed Sundays.

You: What time do you close on weekdays?
Bot: I'm not sure about that. Try asking about: hours, pricing, shipping, returns, or password reset.
     ↑ FAILS: Couldn't match "close" + "weekday" to FAQ keyword


LLM-POWERED CHATBOT:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
You: What are your hours?
Bot: We're open Monday through Friday from 9am to 6pm EST, and on Saturdays from 10am to 4pm EST. We're closed on Sundays.

You: Are you open on Saturday?
Bot: Yes, we're open on Saturday from 10am to 4pm EST.

You: What time do you close on weekdays?
Bot: We close at 6pm EST on weekdays (Monday through Friday).
     ↑ SUCCEEDS: Understood "close" + "weekday" naturally

CONVERSATION 2: Follow-Up & Conversational Context
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RULE-BASED CHATBOT:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
You: How much does shipping cost?
Bot: Standard shipping (5-7 days): free over $50. Express (2-3 days): $9.99. International available.

You: What about overnight?
Bot: I'm not sure about that. Try asking about: hours, pricing, shipping, returns, or password reset.
     ↑ FAILS: No "overnight" keyword in shipping FAQ


LLM-POWERED CHATBOT:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
You: How much does shipping cost?
Bot: We offer standard shipping (5-7 days) free on orders over $50, and express shipping (2-3 days) for $9.99. We also have international shipping available.

You: What about overnight?
Bot: We don't offer overnight shipping, but our express option gets your order there in 2-3 days for $9.99.
     ↑ SUCCEEDS: Understood follow-up in context + made reasonable inference

CONVERSATION 3: Variations & Natural Language
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RULE-BASED CHATBOT:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
You: Can I get my money back if I hate the product?
Bot: 30-day money-back guarantee on all purchases. No questions asked. Contact support@example.com.

You: Is there a warranty?
Bot: I'm not sure about that. Try asking about: hours, pricing, shipping, returns, or password reset.
     ↑ FAILS: "warranty" keyword not in FAQ


You: I can't log in anymore
Bot: Click 'Forgot Password' on login page. Check your email for reset link. Contact support if not received.
     ↑ PARTIAL: Assumes password reset, but user might have other login issues


LLM-POWERED CHATBOT:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
You: Can I get my money back if I hate the product?
Bot: Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee with no questions asked. If you're not satisfied, just reach out to our support team.

You: Is there a warranty?
Bot: While we don't explicitly call it a warranty, our 30-day money-back guarantee means you have full coverage if something goes wrong.
     ↑ SUCCEEDS: Answered related question not in FAQ


You: I can't log in anymore
Bot: I can help with that! If you've forgotten your password, click 'Forgot Password' on the login page and check your email for a reset link. If that doesn't work or you're having a different issue, contact our support team.
     ↑ SUCCEEDS: Asked clarifying follow-up conceptually

CONVERSATION 4: Out-of-Domain Questions
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RULE-BASED CHATBOT:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
You: What's the meaning of life?
Bot: I'm not sure about that. Try asking about: hours, pricing, shipping, returns, or password reset.

You: Do you ship to Canada?
Bot: Standard shipping (5-7 days): free over $50. Express (2-3 days): $9.99. International available.
     ↑ MISLEADING: Matched "ship" keyword but didn't understand "Canada" question specifically


LLM-POWERED CHATBOT:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
You: What's the meaning of life?
Bot: That's a philosophical question outside my area of expertise! I'm here to help with questions about our products, shipping, pricing, and customer support.

You: Do you ship to Canada?
Bot: Yes, we do offer international shipping including to Canada. For specific rates and delivery times to your area, please contact our support team.
     ↑ SUCCEEDS: Specific answer + graceful handling of follow-up


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
KEY DIFFERENCES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RULE-BASED CHATBOT:
  ✓ Simple, predictable, no API costs
  ✗ Rigid - fails on slight wording variations
  ✗ No context awareness - can't follow conversations
  ✗ Can't handle unexpected questions gracefully
  ✗ Requires manual FAQ updates for every scenario

LLM-POWERED CHATBOT:
  ✓ Flexible - understands natural language variations
  ✓ Contextual - maintains conversation history
  ✓ Graceful degradation - handles unexpected questions
  ✓ Scales easily - adapts to new topics in system prompt
  ✗ Requires API key and costs per query (typically $0.0005-$0.001/query)
  ✗ Slightly slower (API latency vs instant lookup)
